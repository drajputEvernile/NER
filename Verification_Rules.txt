Member Verification Guide
 
Base Rules
1. Any two evidences should match to process the record further or it will be
considered as Incorrect Member details.
2. Amongst all three of them, Member Name is mandatory to match. If
Member Name is not given though correct DOB & Member ID given, we
will still reject those orders
3. We need to verify these details in all the pages of record received.
4. System Data May Contain Two Word Name and Three Word Name
 
What If Scenarios
1. "What If…………Incorrect Member received along with the correct Member"
The incorrect Member is received on some of pages in the middle of chart,
then pages must be deleted as per listed below criteria.
Delete Operation
50 or More than 50 pages – Maximum, we can delete 5 Pages 
Less than 50 Pages - We can delete 10% Pages maximum
Move Operation
If we receive multiple Members’ information with requested Member’s
information, we will move the order to Splitting queue on a condition that
the incorrect Member’s information is given either on first 5 pages/last 5
pages.
From above two scenarios, if the incorrect Member count exceeds 10% or 5 pages, we will reject the case due to wrong Member pages.
